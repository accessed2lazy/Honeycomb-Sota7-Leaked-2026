## 🕹️ About

Honeycomb: The World Beyond is a sci-fi survival sandbox set on the alien planet Sota7. Play as bioengineer Hennessy, explore hostile ecosystems, study strange lifeforms, build a research base, and experiment with genetic traits to help secure humanity’s future. This Windows build is an unofficial leaked pre-release: raw, unfinished, and surfaced ahead of schedule.

For solo survivalists, explorers, builders, and science-fiction fans who enjoy unpolished worlds full of experimentation and discovery.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🪐 **Alien Exploration** — Survey vibrant biomes, underground caverns, unfamiliar plants, and dangerous wildlife.
- 🧬 **Bioengineering** — Synthesize plant properties and manipulate animal DNA through experimental crossbreeding.
- 🏕️ **Base Building** — Construct a research base for living, crafting, experimentation, and long expeditions.
- 🔬 **Field Research** — Scan flora and fauna to uncover useful properties and learn how Sota7’s ecosystems work.
- 🛠️ **Survival Crafting** — Gather resources and create the tools and equipment needed to survive the unknown.
- ☣️ **Hostile Environment** — Adapt to alien pathogens, environmental hazards, and creatures that do not welcome visitors.
